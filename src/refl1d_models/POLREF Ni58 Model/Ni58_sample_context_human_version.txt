The data to be fitted is PNR data of a 1200A Ni[58] magnetic layer on a Si substrate
The data format is 3 column ASCII with no header. Columns are Q, R, dR.
There are 2 data files "_u.dat" suffix is spin up data, "_d.dat" suffix is spin down data.
The reflectometry geometry is air/solid - super phase is air, subphase is the substrate, Si.

The data are stitched PNR reflectometry data using the lowest scattering angle of theta=0.25 degrees
The data were reduced at a dQ/Q resolution of 0.01

Hypotheses:
There may be a NiO surface layer
There may be a SiO2 interface layer
There may be magnetically dead layers at both the bottom and top interface of the Ni58 layer
The magnetic interfaces of the Ni58 layer (above and below), may be different from that of the nuclear (structural) profile.
